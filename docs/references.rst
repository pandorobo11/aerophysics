Lee, D. B. and Faget, M. A. *Charts Adapted from Van Driest's Turbulent

Gnoffo, P. A., Berry, S. A., and Van Norman, J. W. *Uncertainty Assessments of

https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110013216
